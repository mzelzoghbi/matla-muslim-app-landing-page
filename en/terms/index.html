Prayer times and religious content

  • Prayer times are calculated on your device from your location and the method you choose. We work to make them accurate, but they are an astronomical calculation that can differ by minutes from your mosque's or your country's timetable; where they differ, follow what is announced locally. You can adjust each prayer by the minute.
  • The Hijri date uses the Umm al-Qura calendar and can differ by a day from the moon sighting in your country.
  • The Qibla direction relies on the device's compass, whose accuracy is affected by nearby metal and magnets.
  • The mushaf text and athkar come from established sources named in the app. If you find an error, write to us so we can correct it.
  • The Fajr alarm and the adhan rely on iOS alarms and notifications; we can't guarantee them if the phone is off, out of battery, or notifications are disabled.
